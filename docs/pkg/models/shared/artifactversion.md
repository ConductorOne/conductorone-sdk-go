# ArtifactVersion

ArtifactVersion is a redacted publication outcome identified by its parent
 and positive numeric ID. It contains no source or executable definition.
 Authorization uses the canonical parent Artifact policy resource; versions
 have no separate entitlement or policy identity.
 READY alone does not grant access: current role eligibility and live parent
 and child records are required, including for a previously published pin.
